## 2. Magnet Press-Fit Cavity & Balance
- **Cavity Geometry:** Each piece has an inverted blind cylindrical bore ($\varnothing 5.20\text{ mm} \times 2.20\text{ mm}$) centered in its bottom base face.
- **Interference Fit:** The 5.20 mm bore provides a $+0.10\text{ mm}$ press-fit interference for standard 5.0 mm diameter N35 magnets. Magnets can be pressed in flush by hand or with a flat wooden dowel—zero glue required.
- **Center of Gravity:** The heavy sintered neodymium magnet (density $7.5\text{ g/cm}^3$) concentrated at the extreme bottom base lowers the piece's center of gravity to just $4.2\text{ mm}$ above the board. This yields phenomenal tipping stability, preventing toppling up to an 82° board incline.

---

## 3. Printing Recommendations
- **Layer Height:** 0.12 mm (adaptive layer height on piece heads for maximum surface smoothness).
- **Infill:** 100% solid infill on base discs (height 0 to 4 mm) for extra mechanical heft, 20% gyroid on stems.
- **Colors:** Warm Ivory / Marble White PLA for White pieces; Matte Galaxy Black PLA for Black pieces.
