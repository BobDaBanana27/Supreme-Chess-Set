# Chunk 08 — Mechanical Enclosure Architecture (FreeCAD Base Chassis)

## Overview & Work Completed
- **3D CAD Modeling:** Modeled the main enclosure base half in FreeCAD 1.1 (`cad/supreme_chess_base.FCStd`) and exported a universal editable CAD standard STEP file (`cad/supreme_chess_base.step`).
- **Internal Structural Integration:** Incorporated the 160.0 mm × 80.0 mm board insert pocket, internal slide-way channel for the slide-out capture drawer, electronics cavity for the RP2040 motherboard and battery, side USB-C access port, and dual Ø5.2 mm magnetic catch latch recesses.
- **Hinge & Travel Envelope:** Formulated the precision knuckle hinge architecture with Ø3.0 mm stainless steel axis pins, delivering a flat 180° open playing stance and a rugged 168 mm × 92 mm × 30 mm folded travel volume.
- **Deliverables Produced:**
  - `cad/supreme_chess_base.FCStd`: Fully editable native FreeCAD model.
  - `cad/supreme_chess_base.step`: Standard neutral 3D CAD STEP assembly file.
  - `docs/MECHANICAL.md`: Mechanical dimensions, tolerances, wall thicknesses, and recommended FDM printing parameters.
  - `assets/hour08_cad_base_chassis.svg`: Engineering drawing featuring orthographic top, front, and cross-sectional views.
