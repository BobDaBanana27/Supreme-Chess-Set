# Chunk 01 — Project Scope, Requirements, & User Stories

## Overview & Work Completed
- **Engineering Focus:** Defined complete system requirements, mechanical tolerances, travel envelope constraints, magnetic retention specifications, and user stories.
- **Problem Formulation:** Traditional travel chess boards lack any automated tracking. Adding computer vision is excessively complex and blows past the $30 budget. Formulated the **Dedicated Capture-Tray Hall-Effect Sensing Architecture**: by embedding individual micro Hall-effect sensors under molded, labeled slots in dual slide-out capture trays, piece identity is inherently encoded by slot position (Queen slot = +9, Rook = +5, etc.).
- **Deliverables Produced:**
  - `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md`: Detailed functional specifications (FR-01 to FR-04), physical constraints, and user acceptance criteria.
  - `assets/hour01_scope_concept.svg`: Architectural layout and concept visualization showing the 160×160 mm board, dual capture trays, and live OLED score display.
