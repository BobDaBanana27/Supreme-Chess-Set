# Chunk 12 — Display & Electronics Caddy CAD Integration

## Overview & Work Completed
- **Electronics Carrier CAD:** Modeled the electronics caddy and display bezel in FreeCAD 1.1 (`cad/electronics_bezel.FCStd`) and generated the neutral STEP file (`cad/electronics_bezel.step`).
- **Display & Control Openings:** Engineered the 26.0 mm × 15.0 mm viewing window for the 0.96" SSD1306 OLED, dual Ø4.5 mm button plunger channels for tactical switches SW2/SW3, and the USB-C charging cutout.
- **Exploded System Assembly:** Modeled the full vertical stackup linking the folding base chassis, PCB sensor strips, slide-out capture trays, board grid inlay, electronics carrier, and 32 magnetic chess pieces.
- **Deliverables Produced:**
  - `cad/electronics_bezel.FCStd`: Native parametric FreeCAD carrier caddy model.
  - `cad/electronics_bezel.step`: Standard neutral STEP CAD file.
  - `docs/ELECTRONICS_PACKAGING.md`: Packaging volume budget, cable routes, and thermal dissipation analysis.
  - `assets/hour12_cad_assembly_exploded.svg`: Complete 6-stage exploded CAD architecture diagram.
